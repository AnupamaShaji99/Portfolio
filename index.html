🚆 Public Transport Patronage Recovery in Post-COVID Victoria

Analysed Victorian public transport demand from 2018–2025 to understand how travel behaviour changed after COVID and identify implications for future service planning.

Key Finding: Patronage stabilised at approximately 84% of pre-COVID levels, with weekend travel recovering more strongly than weekday commuting.

📊 ABN Data Quality Assessment

Transformed large-scale XML business registry data into a reporting-ready dataset and assessed postcode completeness, validity and geographic coverage.

Tools: Power BI, Power Query, DAX, XML
