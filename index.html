🎓 Student Follow-Up Prioritisation & Enrolment Support Analytics

Inspired by my experience supporting student conversion processes, this project combines SQL analysis, Power BI visualisation and predictive modelling to identify progression barriers, workload drivers and students requiring prioritised follow-up support.

Business Problem: Student conversion teams manage large applicant pipelines and must prioritise follow-up activities effectively. This project provides insights into progression patterns and supports data-driven intervention strategies.

Key Findings: T1 Enrolment generated the highest follow-up workload, applicants demonstrated the lowest progression rates, and progression outcomes declined significantly after repeated contact attempts.

Tools: Power BI, SQL, Python, Pandas, Scikit-Learn, DAX
