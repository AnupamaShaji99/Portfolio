### Public Transport Patronage Recovery in Post-COVID Victoria

**What I wanted to uncover**

Has public transport demand in Victoria recovered to pre-COVID levels, and what travel patterns have changed since the pandemic?

**Why this matters?**
To help transport authorities identify the gap between pre and post COVID levels of public transport usage in Victoria.These insights help support service planning, resource allocation and investment decisions across transport networks.

**Tools used**

Tableau • SQL • Excel

**Key Findings**

- Public transport patronage has not fully returned to pre-pandemic levels across all modes.
- Recovery is strongest during weekends and weakest during traditional weekday commuting periods.
- Travel behaviour suggests a shift towards flexible work arrangements and non-routine travel patterns.
- Service planning may need to be adjusted to align with changing demand patterns.

### Australian Business Registrar Data Quality Assessment 📊

**What I Wanted to uncover**

How complete and reliable is Australian Business Register (ABR) data, and what data quality issues could affect reporting and analysis?

**Why This Matters**

Business decisions are only as reliable as the data behind them. Missing or invalid information can impact reporting accuracy, stakeholder confidence and downstream analysis.

**Tools Used**

Power BI • Power Query • Excel

**Key findings**

- Around 35% of business records were missing postcode information.
- NSW and VIC contained the highest concentration of registered businesses.
- Data completeness varied across key fields.
- Missing postcode data represented a significant data quality concern for geographic analysis.
